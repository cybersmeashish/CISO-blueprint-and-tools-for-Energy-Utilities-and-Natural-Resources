# CISO blueprint and tools for — Energy, Utilities & Natural Resources

By [Ashish Srivastava](https://github.com/cybersmeashish).

A structured home for industry security blueprints, practical tools, original research and traceable evidence. This repository follows the user-defined industry taxonomy.

**Stage:** sector folders initialized. Across the seven-repository portfolio, BFSI and Oil & Gas retain their existing migrated reference content; other sector blueprints/tools are future contributions. Folder coverage does not assert client engagements, deployment, accreditation or regulatory conclusions.

## Sub-industry workspaces

| Sub-industry | Scope | Workspace |
|---|---|---|
| Oil & Gas | Upstream exploration/production, midstream transportation/storage, and downstream refining/marketing. | [Open folder](Oil%20%26%20Gas/README.md) |
| Energy & Utilities | Power generation, transmission, distribution, water management, and waste services. | [Open folder](Energy%20%26%20Utilities/README.md) |
| Renewables & CleanTech | Solar, wind, hydro, green hydrogen, and battery storage. | [Open folder](Renewables%20%26%20CleanTech/README.md) |
| Mining & Metals | Extraction and processing of minerals, steel, aluminum, and precious metals. | [Open folder](Mining%20%26%20Metals/README.md) |
| Chemicals | Petrochemicals, specialty chemicals, fertilizers, and industrial gases. | [Open folder](Chemicals/README.md) |

## Contribution method

Add material within the relevant sub-industry folder. Document decisions, runnable instructions, primary-source context, tests/observations and limitations. Existing migrated files keep their relative directory layout and original bytes.
